Privacy Policy

What Vytreos does and doesn't do with your retinal photograph.

What Vytreos does today

The web app loads in your browser; no account is required. When you click "Analyze", your retinal photograph leaves your device briefly so the selected AI provider can process it. We do not persist your photo on a Vytreos-owned server today — it's buffered in browser memory, forwarded to the chosen provider, then discarded.

Third-party AI providers

When you click "Analyze", the photo is forwarded to whichever provider matches the model you selected: Google Gemini, Mistral AI, HuggingFace, Roboflow, or Groq. Each has its own data retention policy; by using Vytreos you accept that your photo is processed under their terms at the moment of analysis.

Safer choice: Use a freshly-taken photo, generated test image, or a heavily-cropped sample when you don't actually need a real reading. Don't upload identifiable retinas for casual demos.

Cookies and local storage

Vytreos uses your browser's localStorage for UI state and any future session token. We do not use cross-site tracking cookies. The PWA service worker caches static assets so the app loads fast on repeat visits.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR / CCPA / LGPD / etc.) you have rights to access, export, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise them, email privacy@vytreos.app. For local-only data, your browser's "clear site data" button removes everything Vytreos stored locally.

Children

Vytreos is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

We transport over HTTPS, set strict response headers via vercel.json, cap upload body size, and validate the provider list server-side. No system is perfectly secure — if you find a vulnerability, please email security@vytreos.app instead of disclosing publicly.

We may update this policy. Material changes are noted by date below. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
